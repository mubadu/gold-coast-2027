      Day by day

      The parks, on the quietest days

      Queensland's school year starts on Wednesday 27 January 2027, so in both trips the theme parks land on school days, after the holiday crowds have gone.

      MOVIE WORLD

      The big one: DC Rivals HyperCoaster, the tallest, longest and fastest hypercoaster in the southern hemisphere at 61m.

      Also: Superman Escape, Green Lantern, Batwing Spaceshot, the stunt show and parades. Gates open at 10am.

      Getting there: tram to Helensvale, then the TX7 bus to the gate.

      SEA WORLD

      On the water: dolphins, sharks, penguins and the shows at Main Beach.

      For the riders: the New Atlantis precinct: Leviathan wooden coaster, Trident (a 42m swing) and Vortex.

      Getting there: tram to Main Beach, then bus 704.
